Our Story

My name is Lisardo Warush. I am from Ecuador, in Latin America. I am a single father raising my two sons, Kevin and Leonel, who are 11 and 14 years old and currently attend school.

We do not own a home and currently live in a rented place. I work as a security guard and earn about $500 per month. With this income, it is very difficult for me to afford a home for my children.

We were born in a rural community far from the city. I have worked hard to support and educate my sons. I have also experienced discrimination because of my Indigenous background.

Kevin and Leonel dream of something many people consider basic: a safe, decent and healthy home where they can live peacefully and build a better future.

That is why we created this campaign to raise funds for the construction of their home. Every contribution, regardless of the amount, brings us one step closer to making this dream a reality.

How the Donations Will Be Used

Funds will be used exclusively to advance construction of the home and to cover the materials and necessary expenses required to make it possible.

  • 🧱 Construction materials: cement, blocks, sand, gravel and other materials.
  • 🏠 Roof, walls, doors and windows.
  • 🚿 Basic water and sanitation installations.
  • ⚡ Electrical installation.
  • 👷 Labor and construction work.
  • 🛏️ Basic items needed to make the house safe and habitable.

Your Help Can Make a Difference

Building a house is not only about putting up walls. It means creating a place where Kevin and Leonel can feel safe, rest, take care of their health and look toward the future with hope.

Every donation counts. A small contribution, combined with the contributions of many people, can become a roof, a door, a room and, ultimately, a home.

If you cannot donate, you can also help by sharing this campaign with family, friends and anyone who may be able to contribute.

Thank you for reading our story, sharing it and being part of this effort. ❤️
